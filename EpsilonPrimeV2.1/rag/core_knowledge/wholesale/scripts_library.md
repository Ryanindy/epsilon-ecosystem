
SELLER OPENING:
Hi, this is [Name]. I’m calling about the property at [Address]. Did I catch you at an okay time?

DISCOVERY:
Can you tell me what’s prompting you to consider selling?
What condition would you say the property is in?
If we handled everything simply, what would you hope to walk away with?

CREATIVE PIVOT:
If price isn’t perfect today, would flexible terms help you?
Would monthly income or reduced stress be more valuable than a lump sum?
